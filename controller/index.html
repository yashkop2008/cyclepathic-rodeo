WAITING FOR THE HOST TO START
  1. YOUR PHONE IS THE GROUND. Tilt it and the course tilts with it. Your rider stays upright. Try it on the left!
  2. STEEPER = FASTER... but if the ground under you gets too steep (past ~50°) you WIPE OUT. Watch the balance meter. Mud, sand and slick metal are less forgiving.
  3. LAND LEVEL. In the air, level out the ground you're landing on. Clean = PERFECT speed boost. Crooked = SLOPPY, you lose speed. Way off = crash.
  4. TIME THE OBSTACLES. Swinging, bouncing and flashing things BONK you backwards: speed up or brake to slip past. CRASH = LAST CHECKPOINT, clock still running.
  5. FINISH FIRST and you become a GRAVITY GOD: your tilt tips everyone else's world!
